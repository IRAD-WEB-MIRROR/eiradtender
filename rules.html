NOTICEBids are accepted on paper only, in a sealed envelope, at the address of the procuring entity before the deadline (Astana time, UTC+6). Electronic bidding will open in 2027. The portal is updated each working day by 18:00.

Rules of public procurement

Law
Public Procurement Act 2015 of the ISEZ, as amended in 2021; Regulations on the State Procurement Portal (Order of the Ministry of Economy No. 112, 2016).
Open tender
The standard method. Notice at least 30 days before the deadline (15 days below 1,000,000 som). Lowest evaluated price wins.
Request for quotations
Standard goods and services up to 2,500,000 som. At least 10 days; at least three quotations are sought.
Two-stage tender
Complex works and systems: technical proposals first, then prices from the qualified suppliers.
Single source
Only on the grounds of art. 21(1): (a) one supplier in the ISEZ; (b) urgent need after an emergency; (c) additional works under an existing contract up to 20%; (d) state security; (e) natural monopolies. The grounds are published with the contract.
Language
Bids are submitted in the state language. Documents in another language must come with a certified translation (Act, art. 17(4)).
Bid security
1% of the estimate for open and two-stage tenders, by bank guarantee or deposit to the procuring entity's account at Talasbank.
Complaints
Within 5 working days of the result, in writing to the Department's Complaints Commission, Government House, Talas. A complaint suspends the signing of the contract.

From 2027 suppliers will sign in with e-Irad ID to submit electronic bids: id.e-irad.org

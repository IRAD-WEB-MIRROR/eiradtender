Announcements

28.10.2026

Electronic bidding from 2027: seminars for procuring entities

From 16 November to 18 December 2026 the Department holds seminars on electronic bidding for the staff of procuring entities at Government House, Talas, and at Nurzhan City Hall. Until electronic bidding opens, bids are accepted on paper only.

21.09.2026

Reminder: bids in the state language

Under article 17(4) of the Act, bids and the documents attached to them are submitted in the state language. A document in another language must be accompanied by a certified translation. Bids that do not meet this requirement are rejected at opening.

01.07.2026

List of unreliable suppliers updated

A supplier on the list may not take part in public procurement for two years (art. 43). Procuring entities must check the list before signing a contract.

03.11.2025

New version of the portal

From 3 November 2025 the portal publishes, for every procurement, the bids received and the contract signed, as well as the register of suppliers and the list of unreliable suppliers. Notices published before this date are kept by the procuring entities.

01.07.2021

Amendments to the Public Procurement Act

The amendments to the Public Procurement Act 2015 enter into force. Request for quotations may now be used for purchases up to 2,500,000 som. The Rules page has been updated.

30.03.2020

Bids during the quarantine regime

During the quarantine regime bids in sealed envelopes are accepted at the entrance of the procuring entity's building. Bids are opened without the public, and the minutes are published on the portal.

01.02.2016

The State Procurement Portal opens

Under the Public Procurement Act 2015, notices of open tenders and requests for quotations of the state bodies of the ISEZ are published on this portal. Bids are submitted on paper at the address of the procuring entity.
